**Two metrics are missing and it is the host, not the track.** The
playback ladder hit objective 0c - the macOS `close()` wedge - and the
settling metric could not then reopen the port. That is a documented
macOS defect, seen once here and not on Track B today; one observation
is not evidence that Track A is more prone to it.
