The #5 glitch by wait state

At flash wait state 6 the glitch knocks a handful of samples about 30 codes off their level, at the same six points in every cycle on all three benches. At 4 and 5 it is barely there. The severity number adds all of it up. This page shows what goes into that number — and, in the third section, how much of it the records never wrote down.

  • ADC 12-bit, codes 0–4095
  • DAC table 256 levels, each held 2 samples
  • one cycle = 512 samples
  • 24 runs per wait state per bench
  • preset =200000,200000M

How a glitch becomes a number

Each DAC level is sampled twice, so the two samples should agree. Subtracting them cancels the waveform and leaves noise, plus any sample the glitch pushed off its level. Averaging those differences over hundreds of cycles, position by position, gives a 256-point profile. Severity is the sum of every position's distance from the profile's median.

The profile on windows-desk, at each wait state

The same vertical scale on all three rows, in codes. At FWS 6 six positions stand about codes off the floor. At FWS 4 the sites are a few codes tall. The shaded floor is everything else, and at this scale it is thinner than a line.

What the record could not say

issue5_sites.py stored each run's six strongest sites and nothing about the rest. At wait states 4 and 5 that was almost always the whole list. At wait state 6 it never was — every row of every bench, 120 runs, had more sites than it could write down. The dropped total is still recoverable, because the severity was summed over all of them before the list was cut.

The sixth stem is a slot, not a position

Two positions can look mutually exclusive when they are only competing for the last place in the list.

Where the severity lives

The share of each run's severity that sits in the flagged sites, median over 24 runs. At FWS 6 it is most of the number on every bench. At FWS 4 it is a small part of a small number, and the rest is the noise floor.

Which positions, on which bench

How many of 24 runs drew a site at each cycle position. At FWS 6 the same six positions come up on all three benches, most of them in every run. At FWS 5 positions 155 and 251 appear on all three and the rest differ. At FWS 4 the benches share little beyond 240.

Severity run by run

Each dot is one run's severity; the tick is the median. Each wait state gets its own horizontal scale, because FWS 6 is ten times FWS 4. The windows-desk FWS 5 spread comes from one early block. Its repeat session is marked with a ring.

The sites are not scattered — they are a comb

At wait state 6 the large sites — the ones about 30 codes tall, against a second population near 2 — lie on a lattice of period 21: 12, 33, 54, 96, 117, 138, 159, 180. Folded onto that period they land near one phase on all three boards.

Corrected once whole site lists existed. This page first reported R = 1.000, and that figure came from the six sites a truncated row stored — which are by construction the six strongest and happened to all lie on the lattice. Measured on the true large population, 9 or 10 positions, two of them are off it: 169 (mod 21 = 1) and 247 (mod 21 = 16). So R is 0.75 to 0.94, the comb is 8 of 10 rather than all of them, and its significance is decisive on one board (p = 6e-04) and marginal on the other two (p = 0.07). The full strong set is not a comb at all — 30 to 65 positions per arm, mostly the 2-code population, R below 0.4.

What it predicts, and what would refute it

A disturbance periodic in wall time averages away under a fold over hundreds of cycles. One that survives is locked to the table index. And because 21 does not divide 256, a free-running period-21 disturbance would land four positions further on at each wrap and smear too — so surviving the fold also implies it is re-synchronised when the table wraps. No mechanism is named here; that is what the stored profiles are for.

The jumper crossover: does it follow the wire or the board?

The three boards share one desk, so the loopback wires could be exchanged between two of them rather than refitted on one — and then put back. The third board was never touched and is the drift control. Under the material hypothesis the two swapped boards must trade values; a board-bound difference cannot imitate that.

What it says

Two results, and they must be reported apart. The large between-board difference stayed with each board through both legs — the registered statistic came out NEITHER, so the 9.6% iron-versus-copper gap is not the jumper material. Separately, each board shows a small reversible response to its own specific pair of wires. Reversal excludes re-seating: a fresh contact cannot reproduce five positions to within 0.15 codes. But one pair of each material cannot separate this pair — its length, routing, crimps — from the metal, and no further arm of this design can. Blended, the two read as the material hypothesis again.

What this page cannot say

  • Full profiles are not stored, and until 2026-09-13 neither were full site lists. Rows kept at most six sites plus two totals, so the non-site positions appear only as a derived floor and the dropped sites only as a total. issue5_sites.py now stores every site and the whole 256-point profile; rows taken before that cannot be recovered, only re-taken.
  • The comb is measured on the visible half. At FWS 6 the six positions that make R = 1.000 are the six a row had room for. If the sites those rows dropped are off the lattice, the comb is weaker than it looks — which is exactly what the next arm settles.
  • Three benches are three boards. A difference between benches is board, jumper material or the USB traffic on the device. windows-desk's jumpers are iron and the others' are copper. Nothing here separates those.
  • mac-bench is shown probe-free, session 2. Its first arm had scope probes clipped to A0 and A1, which moved every severity figure and none of the FWS 6 sites.
  • `total_abs` is not a usable statistic at wait state 6, and the control proves it. The untouched board's severity median went 398.1 → 359.2 → 362.1 across the session — a 10% swing with nothing done to it — while its comb sums moved 0.43. Every cross-bench severity figure this campaign started from was reading that.
  • The severity figures are unaffected by the truncation. total_abs is threshold-free — it sums all 256 positions and never consults the site list. What the truncation costs is the site set, not the size.
  • Byte conservation is unrelated. None of this touches the 0h or #80 results, which never read the analog path.
