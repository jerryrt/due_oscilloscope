Own model, written for this project (whatif/sw3157, 2026-11-11): a 3157-class SPDT analog switch used as an SPST range
switch, and the TMUX1119 for comparison, each as Ron(V) from a digitised typical curve scaled to datasheet corners.
Generated by make_lib.py (this folder), which carries the traced points; curves.json holds them as data.
Datasheets (vendor files, git-ignored; fetched and sha256-checked by tools/fetch_models.py from models/manifest.json):
  models/74lvc1g3157/74lvc1g3157.pdf  Nexperia 74LVC1G3157 Rev. 11, Table 8 (p. 6) and Fig. 10 (p. 8)
  models/ts5a3157/ts5a3157.pdf        TI TS5A3157 SCDS219D, Sec 5.5 (pp. 6-7)
  models/tmux1119/tmux1119.pdf        TI TMUX1119 SCDS401C, Sec 5.6 (pp. 5-6) and Figure 5-1 (p. 9)
Every figure used is quoted in the .lib header; assumptions are marked *(assumption)*.
