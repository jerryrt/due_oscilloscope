## Gaps (what this package could not source)

- Renders are made by `kicad/render.sh` in the PCB image: a copper-and-silk top view and a bottom-copper view (SVG rasterised with the image's `svg2png.sh`), the two 3D renders (top, bottom) and one crop of the top silk per overhang band (the Due's pin names). Section 1 says whether each is newer than the board.
- The capacitance extraction was re-run on this board (identical to the first run on `v1_merge5`); the scope / AWG study (`v1/results/`) takes its figures from it and is unchanged; `v1/results/*.txt` are the logs read here. The IN-net and AWG_BPL figures (own scripts, coarser cells) were taken on `v1_merge5` and not re-run *(check)*.
- The ripple evidence for the LM27762 capacitor placement is v2's pw2_ac model run with v1's measured distances (`v1/results/pw2_ac_v1.json`, `v1/sim/pw2_ac_README.md`); its figures are in the LR-5 deviation row (section 5), not in a section-6 row *(check)*.
- The spec team's rulings (the silk waivers, the C301/C304 exception, the power caps and the in-band ripple fallback, R517-R519 under D24, the 0.45 mm edge clearance, the outline and pin-label ruling, R109's removal, the unequal sense legs) are recorded in `kicad/gate3/notes.md` (the running record, kept with this package); the MH602 caliper check is the gate brief's statement, not a repo file *(check)*. - Prices: every ladder was read 2026-11-11 / 12 and not re-read; C21189 was never read (priced from the international read of v2, marked D); the PCB is a published rule, not a quote; the owner-fitted headers are priced from v2's three retail listings; the M2.5 standoff and screws are unpriced.
