# AFE version 2: the scope channel (design note)

Two channels on one TPH2502 dual, unity followers on +3.3 V only (V- = ground), the input network of
`../../budget/v2_scope.py` with the REF3030E as the centre (D-V2-10). Evidence for every number
below is in `report.md` (generated by `report.py` from `results/`; `run.py` regenerates them). The circuit
is `scope_v2.cir`; the parts are `parts.csv`. Figures marked *(check)* are open: see report.md, "Open items and spec questions".

## Circuit, per channel (CH1 is `a`, CH2 is `b`)

```
 BNC --+-- R1a 510k -- R1b 30k --+-- N --- Rnp 470 ---+--- (+) TPH2502 half --+-- OUT --- Riso 22 ---+-- ADC pin (A0 / A1)
       |                         |                    |    (-)--------------+                       |
       +------- C1 22p ----------+                    Cpx 10p -- GND                                  Dp BAV99: GND / pin / +3.3 V_A
                                 +-- R2a 1M -- R2b 510k -- GND
                                 +-- R3a 470k -- R3b 200k -- REF3030E output (3.000 V)
                                 +-- Cn 12p -- GND
```

Follower: the output goes straight to the inverting input (no feedback resistor; RF = 0).

## Decisions

- **No clamp at N.** The amplifier's own input diodes carry +-12 V through R1 at 11 to 15 uA against the datasheet's 10 mA (note 3). A BAV99 at N would
  add 1.5 pF at N (spoiling the compensation by about 1 pF of 26) and a leakage of up to 30 nA *(check: recalled)*, 7.5 mV at N
  and 16 mV at the input against SC-7's 5 mV (calibrated, but not its voltage dependence); the amplifier's own 3 pA is 0.7 uV.
  The BAT54S question does not arise.
- **Bandwidth** (D13, SC-5): no deliberate limit. Rnp x Cpx puts the stage's -3 dB at 40 MHz at the pin, 0.26 dB down at 10 MHz; SM-3's 8 MHz is read at the pin
  (-0.19 dB at 8 MHz with the ADC input attached, worst of three models). The Due's own sampling pole is 7.0 MHz at the derived 3.8 kohm / 6 pF *(check)* and is under 8 MHz whatever drives the pin.
- **Noise (SC-6, D17)**: the noise that reaches the Due's sampling capacitor, through the stage, Riso, the pin and the input switch, 10 Hz to 1 GHz:
  **88 uV** on the vendor macro (9.3 nV/rtHz) and **42 uV** on the datasheet density (6.5 nV/rtHz) at the low-resistance arm (1 kohm / 8 pF), 46 and 31 uV at 3.8 kohm / 6 pF, against 200 uV.
  The switch is not measured *(check)*: the macro reaches 200 uV only below 442 ohm. No band limit is fitted; the one-part limit (Riso 100 ohm and 47 pF C0G) takes the macro's 88 uV to 52 uV and is not adopted.
- **RF = 0.** The datasheet's 25 ohm made peaking worse in the macro (1.10 against 0.81 dB), so none is fitted; Cpx removes the peaking.
- **The compensation step is not trimmed, and it is static.** The 5 % caps and the stray leave a +-5.8 % (95 %) HF/DC gain step above the 13 kHz node pole. The spec lead accepted it as a design call because it
  does not move: 0.02 percentage points over -2 to +4 V of input, a temperature dependence equal to the DC gain's own drift (0.011 pp/K, from the resistors), and C0G has no bias or ageing dependence *(check: EIA class I, no Samsung datasheet in the repository)*. It is
  absorbed by the probe's trimmer (x10) or the host's frequency calibration (SC-5).

## What the layout must do (not in the simulation's gift)

1. **The two channels' high-impedance nodes are N (the R1/R2/R3 junction, C1, Cn, the Rnp pad) and the +IN pad** (dual pins 3 and 5, V- between them). Keep the two channels' copies apart and guarded by grounded copper. The stray budget, in pF, for SC-9's 2 LSB
   at 6 Vpp and 10 MHz (report.md, "Crosstalk (SC-9) and the layout budget it implies"):

   | Between | Budget | Alone, 1 LSB at |
   |---|---|---|
   | the two N nodes | **0.02 pF** | 0.028 pF |
   | the two +IN pads (pins 3 and 5) | **0.02 pF** | 0.019 pF |
   | the two input nets | under 0.37 pF | 0.19 pF |

   The first two add (0.02 pF each reads 1.7 LSB; 0.03 pF each reads 2.6). The 0.05 pF first stated for N alone (1.8 LSB) is the N figure with nothing on the pads. The pads are as sensitive as N, and whether 20 fF between two SOP-8 pins with a ground pin between them is reachable is
   *(check: not in the repository; VT-19's extraction decides)*. If it cannot be held, a lower Rnp (0.76 LSB per 0.02 pF at 100 ohm, 1.05 at 470) or a larger Cpx (0.60 at 22 pF) relaxes it, or each channel gets its own dual (D16).
2. Keep N's trace short: its stray is part of Cn, and 2 to 6 pF is assumed.
3. The dual's supply pin is shared by both channels: decouple at the pin (100 nF + 1 uF).

## What this asks of the rest of the board

- The **REF3030E** carries both channels: it sources up to 4.2 uA each (5.4 uA at -12 V) and sinks about 1.4 uA each with an input at +12 V; its rating is +-10 mA
  and its worst load regulation is 150 ppm/mA, so the two channels move V_REF by under 1.5 uV.
- The **unpowered +3.3 V_A rail** must sink about 36 uA at 0.5 V (PR-3) when both inputs are at +12 V: a bleed of 14 kOhm or less (0.23 mA at 3.3 V if permanent).
  Not part of this design; the rail block must provide it. **Open** (the powercal stream owns it).
- The dual draws at most 2 x 7.5 mA from the +3.3 V_A rail *(derived from IQ max, datasheet page 4)*.

## Reproducing

```
python3 -I run.py            # all sections, about a minute; run.py <section> for one
python3 -I report.py          # writes report.md from results/*.json
```

Sections: `netlist cscan dc ac bw noise d17 tran stab beh prot xtalk static mc guards`. Needs ngspice 47 and numpy; the vendor macro is read from
`../../whatif/tph2502/models/TPH2501_ngspice.mdl` (local, uncommitted) and its sha256 is checked against `models/VENDOR_MODEL.sha256`; the two
datasheet-fitted models (BEH21, BEH60) from `$TPH2502_DIR/beh/tph2502_beh.lib` (default `../../whatif/tph2502`), hashed into `results/meta.json`.
`beh_check.py` is `run.py beh`. `netlists/` and `work/` are generated and may be deleted.
