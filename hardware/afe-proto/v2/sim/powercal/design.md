## 1. The shared rail

**Feedback** (SNVSAF7C eqs 1 and 3, R2 and R4 at least 50k):
V+ = 1.2 (R1 + R2) / R2 = 3.318 V and V- = -1.22 (R3 + R4) / R4 = -2.003 V nominal. Corners: VFB over temperature (+-1.5 %), every resistor at +-1 % and
100 ppm/K opposed over 10 K (EN-1), plus 2 mV for line and load regulation: +3.2205 to +3.4172 V and -1.9549 to -2.0525 V.

| Limit | Rail | Margin |
|---|---|---|
| V2-2: V+ + abs(V-) at the maximum corner against the TPH2502's only supply row, VDD 2.5 to 5.5 V (its abs max is 7.0 V) | 5.4697 V | +30 mV |
| PR-2: the scope op-amp's supply at most 3.45 V | 3.4172 V | +33 mV |
| REF3030E VIN at least VOUT + 0.2 V (SBVS032K 6.3) at the weakest corner | 3.2205 V | +17.5 mV |
| AWG bottom: the weak -rail 1.955 V less 11.5 ohm per rail (the AWG stream's derived output saturation) x 22 mA | about -1.69 V into 50 ohm | the AWG's -1.68 V is reachable with about 10 mV; -1.9 V into 50 ohm is not (hi-Z reaches it) |

**Why the margins are this thin, and what it costs the AWG.** Basic 1 % feedback resistors and the LM27762's VFB tolerance make the +rail a +-2.8 % window and the
-rail +-2.4 %. The rating wants the sum small and the REF3030E and the AWG's bottom want both rails large, so the three limits leave about 70 mV in all, split
as above. `report.md` lists R3 alternatives: 62.2k (56k + 6.2k) gives 56 mV of rating margin but a -1.931 V weak rail; the 68k of an earlier draft fails the rating by 18 mV.
**The AWG stream should state which of its bottom-end figures it needs: if the window bottom must be -1.9 V into 50 ohm, the rail pair cannot be made from 1 % Basic feedback parts.**
The TPH2502 has no separate recommended-conditions table: its electrical-characteristics VDD row (5.5 V) is the figure used *(check against the vendor)*.

**PW-1.** Loads on OUT+ and OUT-, every IQ at the datasheet maximum (7.5 mA per half, TPH2502): scope dual 15 mA; AWG dual 15 mA on each rail; the AWG's offset path through Rg2
(from `../awg/design.py`, read at run time); its output current; ADS1115 300 uA; REF3030E 37 uA; the ladder 1.5 mA; the sense divider's default tie; two scope centres; I2C pull-ups
1.4 mA if fitted on the AFE; the bleed. The 5V pin carries OUT+ plus OUT- x 1.10 plus the 0.39 mA IQ (the inverting pump's input current is its output current; the 10 % is an
allowance, the datasheet's efficiency table is not in the PDF text *(check)*).

| State | OUT+ mA | OUT- mA | 5V pin mA |
|---|---|---|---|
| AWG hi-Z at the window top | 34.9 | 16.2 | 53.2 |
| AWG into 50 ohm at the top | 56.2 | 15.0 | 73.1 |
| AWG into 50 ohm at the window bottom (-1.9 V): **worst normal** | 39.3 | 39.5 | **83.2** (limit 100) |
| fault: BNC shorted, DAC at the 3.3 V reset level | 100.7 | 15.0 | **117.6** (exempt from 100; total with the Due 417.6 of 450) |

The AWG's offset path (read from `../awg/design.py` at run time: Rf 470, Rg2 510, no Rg1, Rvr 0, sha256 in report.md) draws about 5 mA from the +rail through Rg2 and the op-amp sinks it into the -rail: the bottom state costs 39.5 mA on OUT-.
LM27762 dissipation at 5.25 V in the worst state is 299 mW, 104 C junction at 85 C ambient on the datasheet's 62.2 C/W (limit 125) *(check on the board)*.
PW-6: at the 4.50 V minimum the +LDO keeps 1.08 V and the pump 2.35 V of headroom.

**PW-2 and the switching-frequency rule.** The pump runs at 1.7 to 2.3 MHz (2 MHz typ) and cannot be set; it sits inside SM-3's 8 MHz path. The rule as stated: the line
is at 2 MHz +-15 %, identified and not a moving spur *as long as the pump runs at constant frequency*, which the AWG dual's own 15 mA per rail guarantees at any state
(the datasheet gives no PFM threshold *(check)*). Ripple on OUT+ from TI's model is 0.65 mV peak to peak against PW-2's 20 mV; through an *assumed* op-amp PSRR (80 dB to 10 kHz, then 20 dB per
decade; the TPH2502 gives only the DC figure) it is 13 uV at the scope pin against SC-6's 200 uV. The model has no LDO loop and under-reads (version 1's finding); VT-8 measures it.
**With and without the AWG's 1 uF per supply pin (D22, AW-6 now 0.3 mV):** the AWG's supply branch has no filter resistor, so the pin sees the rail's ripple: 0.65 mV pk-pk without the 1 uF, 0.19 mV with it (the AWG stream's factor of 0.3), both far inside PW-2's 20 mV; through the AWG's op-amp-like PSRR row (ripple x 2.65 / (120 MHz / f)) that is **10.1 uV rms without and 3.0 uV with, against 300 uV**: dropping the 1 uF costs this block nothing, and a factor of 10 on the model's under-read still passes.

**PW-7.** Rails reach 90 % in under 1 ms in TI's model, the REF3030E settles in 2 ms (to 0.1 %, SBVS032K), the scope node's own RC is about 12 us: **2.5 ms against 15 s**. What takes seconds is thermal (VT-18).

**SC-9 through the rail (VT-20).** The scope is on +3.3 V alone, V- = ground, so the AWG's negative-rail current never touches it directly. It still hears the AWG by four paths:
(1) the +rail's load regulation (11 uV/mA x the AWG's 16 mA swing = 0.18 mV) through the dual's 80 dB DC PSRR; (2) the 5 V feed (12 mA swing across 1.2 ohm) through the +LDO's 1.9 mV/V and the PSRR; the same current is the only
way the *negative* rail's load reaches the scope; (3) the shared REF3030E: the sense divider's default tie changes the reference's load by 0.165 mA, 7.4 uV, into the scope's centre; (4) the shared return: the AWG's 20 mA across
**an assumed 10 mohm** of plane *(check, LR-1, VT-20)*. Sum **0.12 LSB** (1 LSB = 1.73 mV at the input), 0.116 of it the assumed plane. The step's peak is a few uV at the pin.

**LM27762 on TI's transient model (`lm_tran.py`).** One case converged, start-up at 5.25 V into the bottom-state loads with the feedback set for the maximum corner (OUT+ 3.4177 V, OUT- -2.0553 V settled; +rail 90 % at 0.29 ms, -rail at 0.50 ms; peak of OUT+ 3.495 V and OUT- -2.082 V; **the bare sum peaks at 5.499 V** during start-up, a transient against the 5.5 V steady-state VDD row and the 7.0 V absolute maximum; 20.7 mV of ripple on VIN, 0.16 mV on OUT+, pump 1.39 MHz). The 5.0 V start-up, the 4.5 V start-up (one died on a race over the shared patched library, fixed, then not finished), the ripple case and the AWG step did not finish in 22 minutes of CPU and were killed: they are failures of the model (about half of version 1's runs failed too), not results. The step and the ripple/SC-9 figures therefore rest on the one converged run (ripple) and a hand estimate (step: 1.5 mV peak at the rail), both through an assumed PSRR *(check, VT-8, VT-20)*.

## 2. The unpowered state (PR-3)

## 5. The sense divider and the jumper

```
 A (AWG at the BNC / tee) --Rt 15k-- T0 --Rb 10k-- B --Rd 1k-- REF3030E (permanent default)
                                      |            |
                                      Rs 4.7k      jumper wire: B to the 1/3 ladder tap (ADS input 3 also reads it)
                                      |
                                  ADS1115 input 2
```
k = Rb / (Rt + Rb) = 0.4 and T0 = kA + (1 - k)B. Divider + Rd = 26 kohm, a permanent 0.19 mA at most on the AWG node (through the 50 ohm back-termination, 0.2 % of gain, read by the sense itself).

**Bounds restated for k = 0.4.** Wire off (B = REF through 1k): T0 = 0.4A + 0.6 (3.0 - drop) is 0.88 to 2.70 V over A = -1.95 .. +2.28 V. With the wire to the 1/3 tap: 0.14 to 1.89 V (tolerances and the tap's loading included).
With the wire on, B is the 1/3 tap pulled toward the reference through Rd by the divider's current, about 1.5 V at the window bottom, so the tap's minimum is 0.4 x (-1.95) + 0.6 x 1.5 = 0.14 V. **k = 0.5 would put the tap at -0.21 V (scanned: it fails), so k = 0.4**; the 3.3 V limit is met for any B up to the rail.
**The wire to the 1/3 tap is the choice** because it is the lowest measured node: the offset term (1 - k) B / k that the ADS1115's channel match turns into error is smallest there. The 2/3 tap was worse at the same k (0.96 % gain in the scan).

**One manual action, the sweep with the wire on throughout.** Wire off at the start (tee level 2.0 V): T read on input 2, REF on input 1. Wire on with the tee cable: T and B (input 3) read at the same level. The host solves (A, k) from the
two with the nominal network (Rd, Rs, Zcm included), then every sweep point is A = (T0 - (1 - k) B) / k with T0 undone from the pin reading and **B read at every level** (the wire loads the 1/3 tap by up to 631 mV; because B is measured, the loading is not an error).
The loaded tap is **not a calibration point while the wire is on**; the 2/3 tap, the reference and ground remain, and the wire-off readings before and after read every tap. At the end, the host removes the wire with the tee and reads T at the ratio level again.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Tap range, wire on, every AWG level -1.95 .. +2.28 V, 1 % resistors, REF +-0.1 % | 0.141 to 1.891 V (limits 0 to 3.22): PASS |
| Tap range, wire off (normal use) | 0.881 to 2.697 V: PASS |
| Wire left on after the calibration or missing at the start: tap difference between the positions at every level and tolerance draw | at least 734 mV against a 50 mV threshold: detected; left on in normal use the tap stays in range and nothing is damaged |
| Every position the header allows (GND, six taps, +3.3 V_A; the Due's 5V and VIN as stress): current into the ADS1115's input diodes through Rt || Rb + Rs | at most 429 uA, against the datasheet's 10 mA; the host takes no action |
| Distinguishability: dT = (1 - k)(X - 3.0 V) between neighbouring nodes | 180 mV smallest gap against a 3.1 mV resolution; the top tap reads as "no wire" and the host stops either way |
| **Ratio accuracy, the AWG level through the sweep, 99.9th percentile, ADS1115 channel match 0.1 % max** | **0.489 % gain, 6.87 mV offset: FAIL** against this design's 0.05 % / 3 mV |
| the same at the typical match 0.05 % / with no channel mismatch | 0.336 % and 4.9 mV / 0.246 % and 3.8 mV |

Protection is a single 4.7k in series with input 2: **no clamp diode**, because the ADS1115's own diodes conduct (at GND - 0.3 V) before any silicon clamp would, and a Schottky (the Basic 1N5819WS) is a power part whose reverse leakage at 3 V is not in any datasheet read here *(check)*. Whether the other three inputs read undisturbed during an overdrive
(SBAS444E 7.3.1 warns they can be affected) is not in the datasheet: VT-22 decides *(check)*.

**Why the ratio fails, and it is not the wire.** The tap is read as a ratio to the reference, so the converter's gain cancels; what remains is the channel match between input 2, 1 and 3 (0.1 % max, 0.05 % typ), the 3-LSB offsets, the 1 % Rd that sets B when the wire is off, the 6 Mohm input impedance (typ only) loading a 15k source, and a 0.4 divider that
amplifies every error on T by 1/k and on B by (1 - k)/k. Removed one at a time (`report.md`): channel match 0.39 %, 1 % resistors 0.17 %, Zcm 0.15 %, offsets 0.10 %. They add. The best of 60 divider designs scanned (k 0.15 to 0.5, Rd 47 ohm to 10k, both ladder taps) is 0.43 % and 5.6 mV.

**The staircase with the ADS1115 free-running (D21).** The converter reads the sense node only, continuously; the reference and B (the wired 1/3 tap) are read at the first and last step (one channel switch each, one conversion to settle: "single-cycle settling", SBAS444E 7.3.6, conversion time 1/DR). Because the network is linear, the tap is a straight line of the AWG level and the two end readings fix it:
the sweep's accuracy is **unchanged (0.489 % and 6.85 mV against 0.489 % and 6.87 mV with B read at every level)**. Per level, a step lands inside one conversion, which is lost; one more is dropped for I2C alignment *(assumed)*. 128 SPS (7.8 ms) is the fastest rate at which Table 6-1's peak-to-peak noise still equals the rms (125 uV); 250 SPS and up read 148 to 267 uV pp.

| Step time | conversions at 128 SPS | averaged | noise at the AWG node (dithered) | 256 levels | 1024 levels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 s | 6 | 4 | 156 uV | 0.2 min | 0.9 min |
| 0.2 s | 25 | 23 | 26 uV | 0.9 min | 3.4 min |
| 0.4 s | 51 | 49 | 18 uV | 1.7 min | 6.8 min |

The step time is the Due-side averaging (PA-3's 100,000 samples a point is the 0.4 s row, the 6 to 7 minutes of PA-3); this block adds only the two end readings and the wire-off readings before fitting and after removal (a few seconds). The ADS1115 never limits the staircase: it needs 4 averaged readings at 0.05 s a step.

## 6. The ADS1115 and SC-7

15 bits single-ended at +-4.096 V, 125 uV a code (SBAS444E Table 7-1). Noise, Table 6-1: 125 uV rms at every rate to 128 SPS: one LSB, so the table is quantisation-limited; 64 averaged readings referred to the AWG node through k are 39 uV; without dither the worst case is half a code, 156 uV. INL is 1 LSB at +-2.048 V
and 8 SPS only *(check at +-4.096 V)*. Gain error 0.15 % max and drift 40 ppm/K cancel in the ratio to the reference; the **channel match (0.1 % max) and offset match (3 LSB) do not** and are the error of section 5.

**The extrapolation.** The AWG window (-1.9 to +2.05 or +2.2275 V) covers 71 to 75 % of the scope's range; the top 1.27 to 1.45 V is extrapolated. Truth model: the Due ADC's INL as a cubic or parabolic bow (0.5 and 1.2 LSB at the extremes) or as random knots every 8 codes (1.2 LSB), a channel offset of +-0.5 LSB between the scope pin and the ladder pin *(assumed)*, the pin leakage.
99.9th percentile error at +3.5 V, 1024 points (1 LSB at the input = 1.73 mV; 0.1 % of 3.5 V = 3.5 mV; the whole of SC-7 there = 22.5 mV):

| Window top | INL model | A: tee fit alone | B: with ladder anchors (ground, 1/3, 2/3, reference) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.05 V | cubic 0.5 LSB | 0.27 mV | 3.45 mV |
| 2.05 V | cubic 1.2 | 0.64 | 4.14 |
| 2.05 V | parabolic 1.2 | 1.76 | 3.29 |
| 2.05 V | knots 1.2 | 2.28 | 5.68 |
| 2.2275 V | the same four | 0.26 / 0.63 / 1.64 / 2.25 | 3.28 / 4.01 / 3.06 / 5.33 |

**SC-7's fit share holds over the extrapolated range with the tee fit alone (at most 2.3 mV, 0.065 %), and the ladder taps do not help**: with perfect anchors they would cost 1.9 mV against 0.6, and with the ADS1115's own channel match and offset on the two measured taps they cost 3 to 6 mV, more than the INL they would remove. They remain a fault check and a quiet DC source. This rests on a smooth or knot-like INL; the SAM3X's real shape is VT-23's.

**This block's own SC-7 terms (gain %, offset mV):** REF3030E 0.13 % (initial 0.1 %, 15 ppm/K x 10 K, hysteresis, long-term); sense chain 0.489 % and 6.9 mV; extrapolated fit 0.065 %; ladder drift 0.133 %. **Sum 0.77 %, RSS 0.52 %, 6.9 mV, against SC-7's whole 0.5 % and 5 mV. FAIL**, and the scope's own terms come on top.

## 8. What needs the owner or another stream

1. **Sense ratio (FAIL, 0.49 % / 6.9 mV)**: the jumper method with 1 % Basic parts and an ADS1115 cannot meet SC-7's fit share. Options: relax the sense's gain share to about 0.5 % and accept SC-7 at 0.5 % + the others (fails); fit one 0.1 % pair for Rt, Rb, Rd (Extended, breaks V2-3); or calibrate the AWG against a bench meter (VT-21 already compares).
2. **Ladder drift (FAIL at 10 K)**: recalibration threshold 7.5 K for the ladder.
3. **Rails**: the AWG's bottom-end figure against the rating: about 70 mV to share (section 1).
4. **Contradiction with the AWG stream:** `../awg/design.py` takes the rail corners as 3.235 to 3.342 V and -2.106 to -2.041 V (version 1's 0.1 % feedback). With V2-3's 1 % Basic feedback they are 3.2205 to 3.4172 V and -1.955 to -2.053 V: the +rail is 98 mV wider, and because the AWG's offset is taken from that rail through Rg2 (K = Rf/Rg2 = 0.83), its output moves 81 mV with it. The AWG's window-top and window-use rows should be re-run on these rails, or its offset taken from the REF3030E (its `src='ref'`), which can supply it: its offset path draws up to about 6 mA, 7.7 mA with this block's 1.74 mA, inside the 10 mA rating, but the reference then moves 90 ppm (0.27 mV) with the AWG level and that movement reaches the ADS1115's anchor and the scope centres: re-run section 3's load-regulation row if it is chosen.
5. The AWG's reset-with-short state draws 66.5 mA from OUT+ (supply pin 100 mA with the dual's own 15 mA and the scope's): that is the AWG stream's PW-3 row and is in the 117.6 mA total here.
