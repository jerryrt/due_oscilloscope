## Change against version 1, per block (domestic, per board, same method both sides)

A block's cost is its parts at their order tier, its share of the loading fees, and its SMT joints; the per-order setup fee, the PCB and the owner-fitted parts are their own rows, and the X-ray fee is in the Rails row (the LM27762 is the one leadless part). Blocks are disjoint here: version 1's own table in `docs/afe-v2.md` (10.51, 4.79 + loading, 5.70, 8.26, 0.97, 0.79, about 0.4, international USD, parts only) counts the ladder in both the precision-resistor and the ladder rows, so the two are not directly comparable. Definitions: *Op-amps* = op-amp ICs. *Precision resistors* = version 1's six Extended 0.1 % types except the ladder, and the version 2 Basic parts that do those jobs (scope R1 R2 R3, AWG gain and 50 ohm, feedback and sense dividers; `blocks.csv`). *Switches* = TMUX1119 with its decouplers, pull-downs and clamps. *ADC and reference* = the two ICs. *Ladder* = the eight resistors. *Rails* = the LM27762. *Mid-rail* = version 1's per-channel network (4 x 1R, 4.7k, 1 uF, 6 x 10 uF, 100 nF) with its supply nodes; version 2 has none (the centre comes through R3 from the REF3030E, which is counted under precision resistors).
