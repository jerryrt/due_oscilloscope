## Notes and flags

- **Source: the final board.** Lines are the footprints of `v2/layout/integrated/afe-v2-integrated.kicad_pcb` with `Fitted by = JLC` and not DNP (90 footprints), grouped by the board's LCSC field, designators in the board's own numbering. Owner-fitted footprints (`Fitted by = owner`, 13) are the owner table; test points are not parts. DNP, not ordered: R514 0R.
- **Why the earlier order table lacked the 13 bridges:** the engine's v2 lists (the three streams' `parts.csv` plus `extra.csv`) are the schematic-era block lists and carry no 0 ohm bridge BR101-BR511 (they are layout objects, `v2/kicad/design.py` `BRIDGES`), so C21189 was never summed. The order now comes from the board. Engine lists against the board, per LCSC (the schematic-era lists differ from the board in): C21189: board 15, engine lists 2 (+13).
- Prices and fees are the engine's (ladders in `catalog.csv` / `intl.csv`, fee model in `bom.py`); only the part list changed.
- Owner-fitted headers are a retail listing (EUR, no quantity break, shipping excluded), the BNC a domestic ladder; the M2.5 standoffs and screws, and J501 (CAL pin strip), have no priced part in the repo and are unpriced.
- PCB at 410 CNY/m2 (jlc.com notice of 2026-09-21, read 2026-11-12) + 20 CNY engineering fee over the board outline (`bom.BOARD_MM`, the board as built). A special 20 CNY prototype order (<= 10 cm, 5-10 boards, `server_guide_4114`) was read for v1 but its fit with an SMT order is not stated, so it is not used *(check)*.
- **JLC upload files** from the same board: `jlc-bom.csv` (Comment, Designator, Footprint, LCSC Part #) and `jlc-cpl.csv` (Designator, Mid X, Mid Y, Layer, Rotation); owner-fitted, DNP and test-point footprints are in neither. CPL: positions in mm with Y negated and the board's absolute origin (KiCad position-file / Fabrication Toolkit convention), rotation KiCad's unmodified; JLC's per-package rotation corrections and the Mid X/Y origin were not verified against JLC's viewer *(check)*.
