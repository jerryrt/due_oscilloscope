## (d) BOM delta

Both parts are Basic, so the loading fee is the same (none). Unit prices are the JLC international ladder for both parts (like for like); v2/bom/bom.md prices Samsung domestically at 0.144 CNY (0.0215 USD); the Murata domestic price was not read (check).

Count per board: **v2 = 10** (v2/bom/bom.md line 141: awg C3,C4; powercal C_IN1,C_IN2, C_CP1,C_CP2, C_OP1,C_OP2, C_ON1,C_ON2). **v1 = 11** (whatif/bom.md line 149 in the main checkout: LM27762 CIN 2, CPOUT 2, OUT+ 2, OUT- 2, scope supply branch 1, AWG offset filter 2). v2/bom/v1.csv lists 12 + 12 rows of C15850 in an older midrail set; the 11 is the BOM the cost figures use (ambiguity noted).

## Which rails use C15850 (refs per board)

Shared by v1 and v2 as the same LCSC number; refs differ: v2 lists awg C3,C4 and powercal C_IN1/2 (VIN, 5 V bulk), C_CP1/2 (charge-pump CP), C_OP1/2 (OUT+), C_ON1/2 (OUT-). v1 (11): LM27762 CIN x2, CPOUT x2, OUT+ x2, OUT- x2, scope supply branch x1, AWG offset filter x2. Per-ref bias voltages were not re-derived from the layout here (check); from the BOM text the nominal biases are about 5 V (VIN), the CP and OUT+ rail voltages, and a negative rail at OUT- (whatif/bom.md line 185 mentions -5 V). x1's simulation owns this list.
