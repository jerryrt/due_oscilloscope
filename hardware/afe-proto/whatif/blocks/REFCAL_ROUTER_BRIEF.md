## Flow (do these, in order, and verify each)
1. Build the work board: merge4 + REFCAL's part poses (hundreds 5, designators incl. R520) and in-fence copper from the partial, WITHOUT any +3V3A copper (re-do that with the router) and with R517.1 on /P33_RN; whatif/blocks/port_awg.py is the pattern (poses, reference-text pose, copper wholly inside a fence; REFCAL fence (44.5, 4.6, 64.5, 36.0)). Fix the placement problems of the partial (courtyard overlaps; keep designators >= 1.0 mm on F.SilkS later).
2. Hand-place and pre-route the SENSITIVE nets (VREF legs A, B, C, D, TAPs, SNS_B/SNS_B_N/SNS_TAP/SNS_TI/SNS_BI, AREF, SDA/SCL exits) as far as the partial has them; they are the "pre-routed" fixed wiring the router must keep.
3. Confine the router: with pcbtools.editor.keepout add rule areas covering everything OUTSIDE the REFCAL fence (all layers, no tracks, vias or pour: the corridors and other blocks already hold their copper, the router may not touch them) and a bottom-layer-only rule area over the REFCAL fence (layers='B': no signal tracks on the bottom; the bottom is the GND plane; vias stay possible for GND pads). The editor has set_outline/keepout; rule areas are exported to the DSN as keepouts.
4. Export the DSN, run freeroute.sh, import the SES into a COPY; count unconnected items for REFCAL's nets; iterate placement/pre-routes (bounded: at most 6 router runs) until zero unconnected inside the fence except the edge points that wait for corridor ends that already exist.
5. GND: every SMD GND pad needs its own via within 3 mm (LR-1), vias 0.6/0.3; the router may add them, or add them by hand with e.add_vias; verify. Then silk: 1.0 mm designators for all REFCAL parts (e.set_ref), words 'REF/CAL' and 'ON' at the CAL shunt-on position (J501 pins 2-3).
6. Remove the helper keepouts (all of them), save the board as whatif/blocks/refcal_router/REFCAL_work.kicad_pcb. Then check: pcbtools.fence.check_fence against kicad/wip/v1_merge4.kicad_pcb (block REFCAL) must be clean; `python3 -m pcbtools.check <board> --config v1 --drc` rows: LR-1 (GND vias), LR-5 (R520 and sensitive nets >= 10 mm from U401/C402), LR-6 (REF/ADS reachable from the star), LR-8 (VREF branches), LR-11, LR-12, DRC with the project beside the board: no clearance, short, hole, courtyard or silk items inside the fence; every other row must not get worse than in merge4.
