## Flags

- **Negative rail re-derived, not simulated.** With 768k gone, OUT- R3 =
  (330k+330k+100k)‖330k = 230.1k, R4 330k: -1.22 x 1.6972 = **-2.0706 V**
  (was -2.0733). At the VFB- limits: -2.040 / -2.101 V; bare rails at maxima
  3.342 + 2.101 = 5.443 V (< 5.5 V). AW-1's 50 ohm edge was -1.971 V.
- **R1 = R3 = 760k** (330k+330k+100k): Rin = 760k + 330k‖760k = 0.990 Mohm
  (SC-3 is 1 Mohm); alpha 1/(2+760/330) = 0.2324 (was 0.2311); ideal C1 for
  C2tot 41 pF is 12.4 pF (12 pF: -3.3 %). Moot if +P replaces the divider.
  The three-part chain also shares R1's 30 V across three 0603s (better).
- **AWG Ra/Rb** 16.45k / 18.8k: slope 1.143 (was 1.153); Ra + Rb 35.25k
  (AW-5 improves). Zero-offset condition needs Rf/Rg = 1.143 + 18.8/330 =
  1.200, has 1.2004 (analytic offset under 1 mV). Ca match: (Rb‖Rx)/Ra =
  1.083 against 1.102 before, so Ca ideal moves about -2 % (20 pF stays).
- **Ladder 8 x 249R:** arrangement not stated. The old 3-parallel + 5-series
  pattern gives 83 + 1245 = 1328 ohm, 2.26 mA from the reference buffer
  (budgeted 1.79 mA; RL still above the OPA365's 600 ohm AOL condition); tap
  voltages and impedances change and C1/C2 of powercal need a re-run.
- **Vmid:** one 330k/330k divider and one 10 uF tap are assumed to feed both
  buffers (not stated). 165k x 10 uF = 1.65 s start-up time constant; 0.1 %
  against the 0.05 % powercal's P8-c assumed. Scope sims still assume Vmid at
  50 mohm; powercal's single-buffer loop peaked at 1.17 ohm at 40 kHz.
- **Sense filter 249R:** 8.6 uA (two channels' R3) x 249 = 2.1 mV DC drop
  on the sense node; RC 2.5 ms. Under +P the sense current changes.
- **Ferrite removed:** powercal's P4 ripple margins (bead + 10 uF about
  -54 dB at 2 MHz) were taken with beads; 1R + 10 uF is a 16 kHz pole with no
  inductance, so P4-a/b/c need a re-run. The 1R in each branch also adds
  headroom loss (1 mV per mA).
- **AWG supply filter 22R‖22R = 11 ohm** (was 10): +19 mV at 19 mA against
  AW-1's 70 mV margin.
- **+C:** with the gain stage gone the ±1.25 V range rides on the Due PGA;
  the range pull-downs (D38, D39) and their pins are freed; Ron no longer
  enters any gain.
- **MCP3564-E/ST stock 42 (LOW)**; MCP3564R variants 0 and 14.
- **REF3030AIDBZR (C38423)** is the non-E grade; the design text says
  REF3030E (0.1 %).
- **BAT54S** is a dual in one package; two placements, one diode used each.
- **BAV99** has no C-number in jlcpcb-parts.md's status table; class is from
  jlcpcb.md (all listings Extended). Leaving the footprint unfitted saves its
  fee (last column).
- **49.9R 1206** at 0.25 W against 0.21 W at the stated PR-4 level.
- **IC decoupling, LM27762 capacitors, MCP CH1 divider, CS pull-up:** still
  undesigned (see "Not counted").
