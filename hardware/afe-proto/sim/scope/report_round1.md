Plan step 2b (`docs/afe-plan.md`), redesigned for SC-3's 20-30 kohm uncompensated input. Regenerated by `python3 -I run.py --par` (four ngspice processes, about 4 minutes) or `python3 -I run.py` (one); raw numbers in `results/*.json`, netlists in `netlists/` (each prefixed with its section). **The superseded 1 Mohm compensated design stays reproducible**: `python3 -I run.py --variant legacy` writes `report_legacy.md`, `results/legacy/` and `netlists_legacy/`; its parameter set is `netlib.P_LEGACY`, unchanged.

## Two wirings of the injection switch, because the decided one does not fit this divider

With the switch's S1 on N, its 6-10 pF sits on a node whose Thevenin resistance is now 3.96 kohm instead of the old divider's 40 pF-compensated 178 kohm. The pole falls to **3.61 MHz nominal and 2.52 MHz at the worst corner** against SM-3's 8 MHz, and the switch's feedthrough puts DAC0 onto N: **915 LSB** of AWG at the pin against SC-9's 2 LSB (above 7 kHz on +/-1.25 V). No divider in SC-3's window repairs either; the sweep below shows SM-3 needs a Thevenin of about 1.0-1.4 kohm with the switch on N, an input of 5.5-7.7 kohm.

**Proposal, simulated alongside ('R3'):** the same TMUX1119 placed in the **R3 leg**, D on the R3 end, S1 on the channel's sense node, S2 on DAC0. In normal use the switch is on (Ron in series with 16.7 kohm, 0.02-0.05 %); for calibration it drives DAC0 into N through R3. N then carries no switch, the switch's capacitance sits on the 10 uF sense node, and DAC0's feedthrough lands on that node through Ron. It costs no part and no type. What it changes in the calibration is in `sim/diff/report.md`: it reads the channel through R3 rather than after the divider. Every row below is given for both wirings where they differ.

Negative inputs draw current **out of** the Due's 3.3 V net (-42 uA at -30 V, one input) and nothing flows into the rail. The positive case is the problem. A 22 kohm input lets +30 V push 1.5-1.7 mA into N; it has nowhere to go but the op-amp's clamp into the AFE rail, and the approved 4.7 kohm bleed lets that rail rise to 3.8 V (5.0 V with both inputs), where the op-amps are partly powered and the current reaches the Due through the sense resistors and the ADC pins (700 uA). The rail must sink the fault current at under 0.5 V: **249 ohm (an existing 0.1 % type) holds it at 0.39 V with one input**; 13.5 mA of quiescent drain on the AFE 3.3 V rail (45 mW in an 0603 *(check rating)*; PW-1's 100 mA budget at powercal's 52-57 mA has room). The supply-pin current is then 65-67 uA (sense path, N at 1.1 V) and the ADC-pin clamps carry another 54-56 uA through R2, Vmid, RG, RF and Riso. PR-3 names supply pins; counted with the ADC pins the total is 119-123 uA. Both inputs at +30 V, or 340-680 ohm, fail the rail. No existing part type clamps N to ground without loading it (BAT54S: 10 pF max; BAV99: silicon, the same 0.7 V as the clamps it would join). **What the existing BAT54S can do is sit on each ADC pin** (pin 1 to ground, pin 3 on the pin, pin 2 on the AFE +3.3 V rail), where the op-amp drives its 10 pF through Riso and its leakage costs 44 uV: its Schottky drop takes the pin's fault current into the AFE rail instead of the SAM3X, so with the 249 ohm bleed everything entering the Due, ADC pins included, is about 85 uA (one input), and the unpowered negative excursion of the pin falls from -0.57 V to -0.22 V, which is still 20 mV outside PR-2's -0.2 V at the datasheet's maximum forward voltage *(check: typical is lower; the SAM3X's own -0.3 V abs max is met)*.

## Requirement problems to hand back

1. **The decided injection wiring (N on a source pin) fails SM-3 (3.6 MHz) and SC-9 (AWG coupling, up to about 900 LSB) with any divider in SC-3's window.** The R3-leg wiring passes both at no part cost; it changes what PA-3's calibration measures (sim/diff).
2. **PR-3 needs the AFE rail bleed at 249 ohm** (13.5 mA quiescent; powercal owns the net) and holds for one input only; with a BAT54S at each ADC pin it holds even counting the ADC pins (about 85 uA). Both inputs at +30 V fail at any existing-type bleed that keeps the rail current reasonable. PR-3's text should say one input or both, and whether the ADC pins count. PR-2's unpowered -0.2 V is missed by 20 mV at the BAT54's maximum VF *(check)*, and by 370 mV without it.
3. **PW-1's 'microamps' on the 3.3 V pin is now 29-361 uA for two channels**, signal dependent (R3's leg returns the divider's centring current); the AREF error it makes depends on the Due's 3.3 V net impedance, not measured *(check, VT-8)*.
4. **SC-8 (1 % before calibration) fails on +/-5 V in both the old and the new design**: the range switch's Ron (3.7-8.8 ohm, no minimum given) is in the G = 1 gain. Not changed by this redesign.
5. SC-9's DC term (0.4 LSB) is the shared Vmid; it assumes 50 mohm and goes with per-channel buffers.
