## Two defects in the specified design, found before anything else

The design was first simulated exactly as specified (R2 returned to Vmid, range switch in the RG leg, RF 3.83 kohm / RG 1.00 kohm, no input series resistor). Two requirement-level problems appeared. Neither is fixed by tuning a value; both needed a circuit change, and the changes are what the rest of this report simulates.

1. **The divider does not centre the pin.** With R2 returned to Vmid the node N sits at (1 - alpha) x Vmid + alpha x Vin, with alpha = 0.2358, so at 0 V in the pin is at **1.261 V**, not Vmid (1.650 V). The +/-5 V range then spans 0.017 to 2.678 V: the low side is **16.8 mV** (needs +80 mV), and on the +/-1.25 V range the pin at 0 V is 0.022 V, clipped at the rail. `architecture.md` says 'pin = Vmid + 0.2358 x Vin', which is true only if the divider's far end sits at Vmid / (1 - alpha) = 2.16 V. **Fix used:** a third resistor R3 = R1 = 768 kohm from N to the 3.3 V sense node (the Due's 3.3 V pin through 100 ohm + 10 uF, 4.3 uA per channel), and R2 raised to a 335 kohm leg (330 kohm fixed + the 10 kohm trimmer at its 5 kohm mid-setting). Then N(0) = Vmid for any R2 (the condition is R3 = R1 only), alpha = 1/(2 + R1/R2) = 0.2330 and Rin = R1 + R2||R3 = 1.0013 Mohm. The centre now tracks AREF ratiometrically. It draws microamps from the 3.3 V pin (PW-1 allows that for sensing).
2. **The range switch in the RG leg makes a peaking amplifier.** The open switch's 6 pF (CSOFF) in series with RG puts a zero at 1/(2 pi RF Coff) = 7 MHz into the gain: in the +/-5 V range the response as specified peaks **21 dB** at 24 MHz with a phase margin of **25 degrees** (OPA354) and 18 degrees (OPA365). A feedback capacitor and scaling RF/RG together were tried (RF 3.83k/1k, 1.91k/499, 953/249 with Cf 0-3.3 pF): peaking stayed 10-15 dB, because the zero depends on RF x Coff only. **Fix used:** the switch is moved to **shunt RF** (closed = +/-5 V, G = 1 + Ron/RG; open = +/-1.25 V, G = 1 + RF/RG), RG fixed to Vmid. The open switch's Coff then sits across RF as a pole that only trims the +/-1.25 V bandwidth, and nothing peaks. Costs, listed honestly: Ron (3.7 typ, 8.8 max) is in the G = 1 gain (+1.5 % to +3.5 % of gain in the +/-5 V range with RG = 249 ohm, within the 0.2290-0.2453 window but a part-to-part spread against SC-8 that the datasheet does not bound), RG now carries the output current in both ranges (up to 5.7 mA), and RF/RG are scaled to 953 / 249 ohm (both E96) to keep the +/-1.25 V pole at 28 MHz. **The Vmid source therefore has to hold its voltage under +/-5.7 mA per channel (50 mohm or less); with 1 ohm it adds 6 mV of crosstalk.**

3. A series resistor **Rnp = 680 ohm** ahead of each op-amp + input (not in the specification) limits the clamp current of a fast edge on the input (73 mA without it, case 5). It costs ~2 dB of extra peaking in the +/-5 V range.

## Recommendation

**OPA354.** With the circuit above it passes every case at its worst corner: SM-3 bandwidth 15.2 MHz worst against 8 MHz, SC-6 noise 157 uV against 200 uV, phase margin 53 degrees. **OPA365 fails SM-3** on the +/-1.25 V range: its 50 MHz GBW gives 7.80 MHz nominal and 7.11 MHz at the worst corner against 8 MHz, and no value of RF/RG/Cf helped (it needs a gain-4.83 bandwidth GBW/4.83 = 10 MHz before parasitics). It is quieter (89 uV against 157 uV), which matters only if SC-6 were tightened. OPA354 costs more supply current (5-6.3 mA against 4.6) and its input stage has a crossover near V+ - 2 V, shown below to be harmless here.

**Cost of the choice on the +/-5 V range:** the OPA354 gives a wide, peaking response there (a 5 dB peak near 90 MHz, about 20 % overshoot on a 5 ns edge). It is outside the 8 MHz band, but the ADC folds it in; the noise numbers above integrate it. There is no requirement on peaking, but the calibration note (case 8) must not assume the response is monotone above 20 MHz.

**Undriven-pin memory.** CLAUDE.md records 56 % of A0's amplitude on an undriven pin. For a bare pin the charge-share ratio Cs/(Cs + Cpin) is 0.75 at 2 pF, 0.56 at 4.7 pF and 0.43 at 8 pF with the derived 6 pF Cs; 56 % corresponds to about 4.7 pF of pin capacitance, which is consistent with the datasheet's Ci = 8 pF (derived, not simulated as the bench effect). A driven pin shows a memory of 8.7e-09 of the step instead, i.e. the op-amp (output impedance 20 ohm to 10 MHz, mostly Riso) removes the bucket charge long before the window ends. The two-channel case at 1,022,727 sps single channel has no inter-channel kick at all (the previous sample is the same channel). The shared Vmid and supply coupling is inside these runs: it is why Vmid must be 50 mohm, not 1 ohm (6 mV at 1 ohm, found while building this).

**Range-switch charge injection** (6 pC into the inverting node, TMUX1119 QC): peak 164 mV and recovery to 0.5 LSB in 52 ns on the +/-5 V range, 400 mV and 76 ns on +/-1.25 V. It happens only when the range is changed; the instrument must discard samples for a few hundred ns, nothing more. The datasheet value is for a 1 nF load and is a model gap.

**Read this table with care.** Powered rows are physical: the pin reaches 3.26-3.33 V at +30 V (limit 3.45) and 0.04-0.11 V at -30 V (limit -0.2), so no clamp current reaches the ADC pin and PR-2 holds with 120-190 mV at the top. The unpowered and floating rows contain a **vendor-model artifact**: with its supply at zero the model draws ~3.5 mA through the supply pins and the floating rail goes to -0.4 V, which no real part does when the clamp current flows *into* the rail. Those two columns (pin -0.185 to -0.216 V, rail -0.41 to -0.44 V) are therefore not evidence for or against PR-2 with the Due unpowered; the unpowered PR-2 check is **inconclusive from the model and is closed by VT-5**. What the model does show and what is analytic: the input current is 38-39 uA in every state; with the AFE rail floating that current must go somewhere, and without a bleed it raises the rail (the model cannot show how far, 1 Mohm gave the same artifact value). A **4.7 kohm bleed** (one part with powercal's +3.3 V bleed, which sits on the same net) keeps it at 38.9 uA x 4.7 kohm = 0.18 V (< 0.5 V) and costs 0.71 mA at 3.34 V of the 100 mA budget; 12.9 kohm would be the upper limit. The current into the Due's own supply (through the 3.3 V sense resistor R3) is at most 5.0 uA (limit 100 uA). Whether the AFE's 3.3 V rail can pass the 39 uA back to the Due's 5 V pin depends on the LM27762, which has no usable model: *(check)*, VT-5.

Fast edges through C1 (30 V, powered, source 50 ohm): 5|+30|5ns: 6.4 mA, 5|+30|42ns: 4.8 mA, 5|-30|5ns: 6.5 mA, 5|-30|42ns: 4.8 mA. Node N reaches 8.4 V and -5.1 V (C1/(C1+C2) of 30 V) and is held to the rails by the clamps only through Rnp. Without Rnp the 5 ns edge puts 73 mA into the clamp (guard below). The datasheet gives +/-10 mA as a continuous rating and no pulse rating *(check)*; 6.5 mA for about a nanosecond is accepted with that caveat.

**What fails above +/-30 V** (PR-1: 'only parts the owner can replace on the shield'). The op-amp is not the limit: at +/-200 V the input current is 260 uA, far below its +/-10 mA, which would need ~7.7 kV across R1. The parts that go first are the passives in front of it: **C1** (12 pF C0G 0603, typically 50 V rated) and **R1** (768 kohm 0603, typically 75 V working voltage; dissipation 52 mW at 200 V against 100 mW). Both are soldered, replaceable parts on the shield; the BNC and the op-amp are not harmed. Voltage ratings are typical catalogue values *(check against the chosen part)*. Above about 90 V R1's 100 mW is exceeded by dissipation alone only at ~277 V.

## Case 8: gain flatness the host calibration must correct

Gain relative to 100 kHz, BNC to ADC pin through the 50 ohm source, nominal corner, dB. The gain at 100 kHz is 2.51 % above the 1 kHz gain on +/-5 V (2.52 % on +/-1.25 V): C1 is the ideal for 40 pF at N, but the true capacitance there is about 1 pF more (the op-amp model's own pin capacitance and the wiring; the step vanishes near 41 pF in the table below), so the compensated divider has a step between DC and 100 kHz, about 2.3 % of gain per pF of error at 100 kHz (0.6 % at 10 kHz, where the divider's 17 kHz corner has only partly turned over). Above 100 kHz the divider is flat to 0.01 dB; the rest is the op-amp. At 20 MHz the +/-1.25 V range is 4 dB down on OPA354 (its -3 dB point is 16.8 MHz).

For the calibration note: (a) the +/-5 V range is flat to 0.05 dB through 8 MHz and +0.27 dB at 20 MHz, then peaks about 5 dB near 90 MHz; (b) the +/-1.25 V range is -0.26 dB at 4 MHz, -0.96 dB at 8 MHz, -1.4 dB at 10 MHz and -3 dB at 16.8 MHz (OPA354), so it needs a frequency-dependent correction from about 2 MHz up; (c) the gain differs between ranges by the Ron term (about 1.7 %) and between channels by Ron spread, so each channel and range needs its own gain (CA-1); (d) group-delay variation 0.1-8 MHz is 6-9 ns per range, to be corrected as phase if the ETS reconstruction needs it; (e) the divider's DC-to-100 kHz gain step (+0.55-0.6 % here) follows C1/C2 tolerance and is per board.
