# 2b scope channel: simulation report (1 Mohm compensated divider, 2026-11-11)

Owner, 2026-11-11: SM-5 is dropped and the input returns to 1 Mohm, compensated. This is the first round's design (`report_legacy.md`, `--variant legacy`) with every change approved since. Regenerated by `python3 -I run.py --par` (variant `hz`, the default); results in `results/`, netlists in `netlists/`. The route (c) work stays reproducible as `--variant lowz` (`report_round1.md`) and `--variant lowz2` (`report_lowz2.md`).

- Simulator `** ngspice-47 : Circuit level simulation program`; OPA354 model `OPA354_PSPICE.lib` 48aeb72c445e... (matches SOURCES.md); `models/rangesw.lib`; DESD clamp diode *(assumption)* for the switch pins at N and the unpowered network.
- Vmid: one buffer per channel, both halves of one TLV9062 (owner): powercal's P8-k stand-in (A0 120 dB, GBW 15 MHz, double pole at 40 MHz, Rout 150 ohm) in the design loop (4 x 1R in parallel, 4.7k, 1 uF, 6 x 10 uF + 100 nF); its |Z| peaks at 0.25 ohm near 2.5 kHz here, as powercal reads. Its 2 mV maximum offset is inside SC-2's +/-30 mV centring allowance, which the DC corners apply. Sense: the Due's 3.3 V pin behind 0.1 ohm *(assumption)*, the shared 249 ohm + 10 uF filter.
- Calibration is the BNC tee (owner, 2026-11-11): no injection switch and no MCP pin on N; N carries the divider, C1, C2, the op-amp and 2 pF of strays only (the op-amp's own input capacitance is in its model).
