**Stimuli.** Supply: Due 5V pin 4.4 and 5.25 V (3.4-5.3 V in the sweep), source resistance 0.2 and 1.0 ohm, ramps 1 us / 100 us / 5 ms, soft-start 20 us / 2 ms, three enable orderings, overshoot 0-0.4 V per rail, rails at their tolerance maxima (LM27762 VFB +-1.5 %, 0.1 % divider). Loads: scope op-amps 5/6.3 mA, AWG short 23 mA (step 0 -> 23 mA in 100 ns), PR-4 +-5 V on the AWG output. Ripple: LM27762 5 mV peak at 1.7/2.0/2.3 MHz and harmonics 1/n to 10 MHz, assumed LM2734Y 10 mV peak at 3 MHz. Unpowered: the Due's 5V and 3.3 V pins held at 0 V (a short: the maximum possible current into them), +-12 V on both scope inputs at once through R1 (PR-1/PR-3), +-5 V on the AWG output. Vmid: full-scale input on one channel (its RG current into the shared node), 1 kHz-10 MHz, and a 10 ns current step. Calibration: Monte Carlo 4000-15000 draws per row, seed fixed.

## 2. Result in one paragraph

135 cases, 115 pass, 20 fail. Of these, 58 describe the design as it stands (56 pass; fails: P5-b; P8-d) and 77 are comparisons, previous arrangements and options, tagged `[...]` (59 pass; fails: P8-a [one shared buffer, previous]; P8-a [M-4, one shared buffer]; P8-a [M-5, one shared buffer]; P8-a [sensitivity, one shared buffer]; P8-a [shared, flat 0.5 ohm]; C3-t [+-5 V, 1024 points, k = 1/3 (previous)]; C1-r [4.7k to S1, previous]; C4-d [100 nF alone at REFP0 (ADS124S08 Sec 9.3.3.2)]; C4-d [100 nF + 10 uF direct (BOM review)]; C4-d [100 nF + 1R + 10 uF (montreal5)]; C4-d [100 nF + (1R || 1R) + 10 uF]; LC-A1 [lowcost]; LC-A7 [lowcost] [+-5 V, 1024 points]; LC-A8 [lowcost]; LC-A9 [lowcost]; LC-A10 [lowcost] [MCP6002, CMRR 76 dB typical, +-5 V, 1024 points]; LC-A10 [lowcost] [MCP6002, CMRR 60 dB minimum, +-1.25 V, 256 points]; LC-A10 [lowcost] [MCP6002, CMRR 60 dB minimum, +-5 V, 1024 points]). No limit was relaxed to pass a case. Approved and now the design (2026-11-11): one Vmid buffer per scope channel plus one for the AWG's Rg (SC-9 through Vmid passes, P8-a / P8-f), the 45 kohm negative-rail divider, the buffered-DAC0 AWG, **the ADS124S08 as the precision ADC** and **the BNC-tee calibration** with one TMUX1119 ratio switch on the AWG-sense divider (k = 1/2), 100k from the AWG's Vmid to S1, and 2 x 100 nF at REFP0. The injection T-switches and their C3 rows are retired. The C1-p rows marked as PR-4's +-5 V band are recorded and not judged (PR-4 allows shield damage there).
