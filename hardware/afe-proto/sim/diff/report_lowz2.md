A source asymmetry dRs is a gain error of about (1 - alpha) x dRs / R1 on one channel, which no calibration of the AFE sees: 0.047 % per 10 ohm. Equal sources cancel except through the second-order change of alpha with Rs. The divider's own tolerance and drift give about 2 x 1.54 x (0.1 % + 25 ppm/K x 10 K) = 0.39 % at the corner, the op-amp CMRR term 0.08 %.
