# AFE version 2: the scope channel, simulated

Both scope channels on one TPH2502 dual as unity followers on +3.3 V only, with the input network of
`budget/v2_scope.py` and the REF3030E as the centre. **Generated by `report.py` from `results/*.json`;
`run.py` regenerates the JSON** (`python3 -I run.py && python3 -I report.py`, ngspice 47 and numpy, about a minute). Design values,
reasons and LCSC codes are in `design.md`; the circuit is `scope_v2.cir`.

**Simulator** `ngspice-45.2 : Circuit level simulation program`. **Op-amp models**, three:
the vendor macro `TPH2501_simplify` (`../../../whatif/tph2502/models/TPH2501_ngspice.mdl`, sha256 `256eeb242fe2d960625eb2c8b8d94d71a3e982730a617f01eb8cfcf90322fae1`, checked by `run.py`), and the project's two
datasheet-fitted behavioural models `BEH21` (open-loop output impedance 21 ohm) and `BEH60` (60 ohm), from `beh/tph2502_beh.lib (whatif/tph2502, read-only)`
(sha256 `e5d30338104d9389b942d8c1b708f02e8850c0f4a7a8caba0815ce47856e8d4e`, small signal only, no noise). The macro is the *single-amplifier*
TPH2501, which shares one datasheet with the TPH2502 (`tph2502.pdf` covers TPH2501/2/3/4), and nothing in it couples two channels. It was
**not** written for this study and its figures are therefore marked *(check: no vendor TPH2502 model)* wherever a
result depends on them: noise density (9.3 nV/rtHz against the datasheet's 6.5), distortion, the unpowered input
structure, the rail-to-rail input crossover (the macro has one PMOS pair) and every crosstalk figure. Stability, bandwidth and noise are
read on all three models; the large-signal figures (slew, THD, settling) are the macro's alone.

## Compensation (C1 across R1, Cn at the node, Cpx at +IN) and the x10 probe

Condition for a flat response: R1 C1 = (R2||R3)(C at N), equivalently C1/(C1 + C at N) = a, where **C at N is Cn plus the stray plus Cpx**:
Rnp (470 ohm) is small against the node's impedance above the 13 kHz pole, so Cpx (10 pF, +IN to ground behind Rnp) is
capacitance at N up to its own 34 MHz pole. Cpx therefore takes 10 pF out of what Cn must supply. Section `cscan` tries every one- and
two-part C1 and Cn (Cn may be absent) from the Basic C0G values 10 to 47 pF, with Cpx 10 pF at N, the network capacitance held to 22 pF
(the BNC's 4 pF and 4 pF of margin under SC-3's 30 pF), against the Monte Carlo (5 % C0G, 2..6 pF of stray at N, 1 % resistors), and reports the 95th percentile of the HF/DC step:

| C1 | Cn | parts | 95th percentile | mean | network C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **22 pF (C1653)** | **12 pF (C38523), the design** | 2 | **5.8 %** | -0.5 % | 11.9 pF |
| 22 pF | 22 pF (Cn as it was before Cpx) | 2 | 21.6 % | -17.7 % | 13.7 pF |
| 20 pF (C1648) | 10 pF (C1634, the part Cpx is) | 2 | 6.5 % | -1.3 % | 10.9 pF |
| 30 pF | 22 pF (best of the two-part pairs) | 2 | 5.7 % | -1.5 % | 16.4 pF |
| 18+22 pF | 15+18 pF (best of the four-part sets) | 4 | 3.8 % | -0.4 % | 21.6 pF |

The caps' 5 % and the stray's uncertainty set the spread, not the choice of value; two more parts per channel buy 2 points. With Cpx fitted
and Cn left at 22 pF the step is -17.7 % on average, which no budget absorbs, so Cn is 12 pF. The step is a one-time gain step of the divider above its
13 kHz node pole.

Nominal HF/DC step (ngspice, with Cpx and Cn 12 pF): -0.59 %. Over the Monte Carlo: mean -0.54 %,
95 % within +-6.1 %, 99 % within +-7.4 %, worst -9.6 / +9.2 %.
Input capacitance 15.9 pF nominal (flat to 10 MHz in the sim: 15.95, 15.94 pF at 1 kHz, 1 MHz).

With a x10 probe (9 MOhm || Cp from an ideal source) the response is flat to 1.51 % pk-pk, 10 Hz to 10 MHz, when the
probe's trimmer is set to 1.80 pF (the sweep is in `results/ac.json`): the input looks like a single R || C to the probe, which
is what compensating a probe needs. Out to 20 MHz the figure is 7.0 %: that is Cpx's roll-off, above the 10 MHz ceiling (D13). A probe's trimmer is adjusted per channel (D-V2-9), so it absorbs the divider's step on that channel; the trimmer's range
against a +-6 % step is *(check)* against the probe's own datasheet.

### Is the step static?

The spec lead accepted the +-6 % step from 5 % C0G as a design call provided it does not move: a static step is a gain ratio the host's
frequency calibration (SC-5) removes once, and a moving one is not. Four ways it could move (`static`):

| Cause | Evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Input level | the step at 100 kHz and 1 MHz against 10 Hz, small-signal at DC inputs of -2 to +4 V, three models | spread 0.021 (vendor), 0.000 (BEH21), 0.000 (BEH60) percentage points at 100 kHz; the vendor's is the macro's reading at -2 V in, where the pin sits 0.19 V from ground (its cause is not isolated); the linear datasheet-fitted models read none. Limit used: 0.1 pp (a study choice) |
| Capacitor bias | C0G has no bias dependence (EIA class I) *(check: the Samsung datasheet is not in the repository)*. Control: C1 and Cn made X7R-like (20 % down at 3 V) move the step by 9.6 pp over the same sweep, so the sweep can see a bias-dependent capacitor | the real parts are not that |
| Temperature | C0G +-30 ppm/K, C1 against Cn and Cpx, over 40 K: +0.13 pp. The resistors' 100 ppm/K opposed (the gain drift of SC-7, the same quantity): -0.43 pp over 40 K, -0.0106 pp/K | the step follows the DC gain's drift, which the 10 K recalibration rule (EN-1) already bounds |
| Ageing | C0G does not age *(check: class I, no datasheet read)* | none |

The step is a fixed per-board gain ratio, set at assembly by the 5 % caps, the 1 % resistors and the stray, and not by the signal.

### Noise at the sampling capacitor (D17)

(208 ns, 2e-05 LSB, stress arm 83 ns) and PM 88 deg. It is
**not adopted**: SC-5 says no filter and V2-9 says no margin the requirement does not ask for.

## Protection

**Powered, DC.** The amplifier's input diodes hold N at 3.90 V at +12 V and -0.61 V at -12 V, with
10.9 uA / 15.2 uA in the diodes (the rest runs through R2 and R3 into the REF3030E and ground). **No external clamp is fitted at N.** The datasheet (note 3) allows 10 mA in
these diodes beyond 500 mV past the rail, and R1 limits DC to under 22 uA. An external BAV99 at N would add
about 1.5 pF at N and its leakage times Rp (250 k): at the recalled 30 nA bound that is 7.5 mV at the node, 16 mV at the input against SC-7's
5 mV (a DC offset that is calibrated; its voltage dependence, bounded by the conductance of 30 nA / 25 V, is a
0.03 % gain term). The amplifier's own input leakage is 3 pA max, 0.7 uV. It buys nothing the
diodes already give, so it is not fitted, and the BAT54S question (Extended) does not arise.

**Edge.** Peak input-diode current for a step at the BNC through 50 ohm (the edge couples through C1 straight onto
N, and Cpx holds +IN at ground behind Rnp), by Rnp (the resistor in front of +IN), with Cpx 10 pF and Cn 12 pF:

| Rnp | +12 V, 2 ns | +12 V, 1 ns | -12 V, 2 ns | +30 V, 5 ns (information) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 63.9 mA | 64.2 mA | 82.6 mA | 125.2 mA |
| 470 ohm | 6.6 mA | 6.6 mA | 9.1 mA | 25.0 mA |
| 560 ohm | 5.7 mA | 5.7 mA | 7.9 mA | 21.7 mA |
| 680 ohm | 4.8 mA | 4.8 mA | 6.7 mA | 18.4 mA |
| 1000 ohm | 3.4 mA | 3.4 mA | 4.5 mA | 13.2 mA |

**Rnp = 470 ohm (C23179) keeps a 12 V edge under 10 mA, by 0.9 mA at -12 V**: thin. Rnp's pole
with Cpx is 34 MHz *(derived)*. A larger Rnp buys current margin and costs bandwidth, noise and phase margin, which are not re-run for
560 or 680 ohm.

**Unpowered** (rail floating with a bleed to ground, REF at 0 V, the Due's 3.3 V net at 0 V, the SAM3X pin as one diode each way; *(assumption)*
throughout: the TPH2502 datasheet gives no unpowered behaviour):

| bleed | +12 V both: rail | pin | current into the Due net | -12 V both: rail | pin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 5.13 V | 0.298 V | 0.0002 uA | -0.00 V | -0.000 V |
| 100 k | 2.20 V | 0.296 V | 0.0002 uA | -0.00 V | -0.000 V |
| 30 k | 0.96 V | 0.294 V | 0.0002 uA | -0.00 V | -0.000 V |
| 14 k | 0.50 V | 0.245 V | 0.0000 uA | -0.00 V | -0.000 V |

Both inputs at +12 V push about 18 uA each through R1 into the rail, so **PR-3's 0.5 V rail needs a sink of at least about 36 uA at 0.5 V (a bleed of 14.1 kohm or less) on the unpowered +3.3 V_A rail**, and with none the rail
goes to 5.1 V. That depends on the other loads on the rail and is for the rail
block, not for this design: it is open item 1. The pin sees at most 0.24 V.

**Adversarial arm.** The vendor macro carries a back-to-back diode pair between its inputs. If the real die has one, the
unpowered follower connects +IN to its output through it, and at -12 V both inputs the pin falls to
-0.292 V without a pin clamp (PR-2 says -0.2 V: **fails**) and -0.142 V with the BAV99 at the pin (58 mV inside). A silicon diode
at 20 uA drops about 0.57 V, so the BAV99 only helps because it shares the current with the two other drops; a
Schottky (BAT54S, Extended) would do better and is not needed in this arm. If the die has no such pair, the pin
stays near 0 V and the BAV99 is not needed. It is fitted as a one-part insurance (Basic, about 1.5 pF on the pin, which the stability grid covers),
*(check)*: one bench read of the unpowered pin at -12 V (VT-5) decides whether to keep it.

## Crosstalk (SC-9) and the layout budget it implies

The two channels share the dual, the supply and the REF3030E. The macro is a single amplifier, so the dual's internal
channel-to-channel coupling is not in it; it is injected as an input-referred source at the datasheet's -100 dB at 5 MHz
(rising 6 dB/octave to -94 dB at 10 MHz is assumed *(check)*), and a -80 dB case. Everything below is the pin
reading of the shorted channel for a 6 Vpp (-2..+4 V) sine on the other, in LSB of 3.2 V / 4096:

| Path | 5 MHz | 10 MHz |
|---|---|---|
| shared rail and REF only | 3e-06 | 7e-06 |
| datasheet -100 dB (-94 at 10 MHz) | 0.014 | 0.026 |
| -80 dB (what the datasheet would need to be wrong by 20 dB) | | 0.13 |
| 0.01 pF between the two N nodes | 0.37 | 0.36 |
| 0.02 pF | 0.73 | 0.72 |
| 0.05 pF | 1.82 | 1.81 |
| 0.1 pF | 3.64 | 3.60 |
| 0.2 pF | 7.25 | 7.18 |
| 0.05 pF between the two +IN pads (pins 3 and 5) | 2.08 | 2.63 |
| 0.1 pF | 4.16 | 5.25 |
| 0.2 pF | 8.28 | 10.45 |
| 0.5 pF | 20.42 | 25.72 |
| 0.2 pF between the two input nets | 0.55 | 1.07 |
| 0.5 pF between the two input nets | 1.37 | 2.68 |

The circuit's own coupling is negligible; **the layout's is not**. The high-impedance node set is N (the R1/R2/R3 junction, C1, Cn, the Rnp pad) **and the +IN pad**:
at 10 MHz each sits at a few hundred ohms (the capacitive divider at N; Rnp plus that, against Cpx, at +IN), and a stray between the two channels' copies of either
injects the other channel's full pin-level signal. N to N costs 28 fF per LSB, pad to pad 19 fF per LSB, input net to input net 187 fF per LSB, and the strays add (the two together read
1.72 LSB at 0.02 pF each, 2.58 LSB at 0.03 pF each, 1.81 at 0.05 pF N to N alone). The +IN pad figure matters because the
dual's pins 3 and 5 are two pins apart with V- between them: **the budget is 0.02 pF N to N and 0.02 pF pad to pad, together 1.7 LSB at 6 Vpp and 10 MHz against SC-9's 2**, and the two input nets
under about 0.37 pF. A pad coupling of 0.02 pF is 1.05 LSB on its own; it falls with a lower Rnp (0.76 at 100 ohm,
1.50 at 1 kohm) and with a larger Cpx (0.60 at 22 pF, 1.39 at 4.7 pF), which are circuit levers if the layout cannot reach it. Whether 20 fF between pins 3 and 5 is
reachable is not known: the pin-to-pin capacitance of a SOP-8 with a grounded pad between is not in the repository *(check: field solver, or VT-19's extraction)*.
The AWG-into-scope coupling of SC-9 is not in this block. VT-10 on the first board decides the scope-to-scope figure, as D16 says.

## Reference loading and the SC-7 terms

REF3030E (E grade, `ref30.pdf` 6.5): sink and source both specified to +-10 mA; load regulation 15 ppm/mA source (max), 150 ppm/mA sink (max);
short-circuit 35 mA. Here: per channel the reference sources 4.23 uA at the bottom of the usable range and 5.4 uA at -12 V, and sinks
1.35 uA with an input at +12 V (clamped by the amplifier's diodes), so it is never near the rating, and the signal-dependent
change in V_REF is under 0.5 uV (source) and 1.4 uV (sink) *(derived from the specified load regulation)*. The reference's Zout does not
couple the channels (shared-REF and rail coupling together is 7e-06 LSB). The 19 uV of REF noise that reaches the pin is in the noise total.

**Open (check)s**

1. **PR-3 rail sink** (the powercal stream owns it). Both inputs at +12 V with the Due unpowered push about 36 uA into the +3.3 V_A rail through the amplifier's input diodes; the rail needs a sink of that size at 0.5 V (14.1 kohm or less) and the quiescent cost of a permanent bleed that small is 0.23 mA at 3.3 V *(derived)*. Whether the LM27762 and the other ICs already provide it is not in this study.
2. **The macro is the TPH2501's, a single amplifier, and no vendor TPH2502 model exists here.** Noise density (9.3 against the datasheet's 6.5 nV, both read), distortion (the SC-12 THD figures), the unpowered input structure, the rail-to-rail crossover and the dual's internal crosstalk are not the part's. VT-1, VT-3, VT-5 and VT-10 on the first board close them.
3. **The Due's ADC input switch and sampling capacitor** (3.8 kohm / 6 pF derived, 1 kohm / 8 pF the low arm) are not measured; they set D17's figure and the response at the sampling capacitor. VT-3 and VT-16 with an external instrument close it.
4. **The datasheet gives no input-diode I-V and no unpowered behaviour**; the protection and the PR-2 adversarial arm rest on a generic diode. One bench read of the unpowered pin at -12 V decides the pin BAV99. The PR-1 edge margin (0.9 mA under the datasheet's 10 mA) rests on the same diode.
5. **BAV99 figures** (VF, CJ, IR) are recalled and not read from a datasheet *(check)*; the BAV99 is only at the pin, where they do not matter at these currents.
6. **Parasitics** (stray at N 2-6 pF, across R1 0.1 pF, BNC and trace 4 pF) are assumptions; the two channels' mutual strays (SC-9) are layout numbers, and nothing here says what a SOP-8 can reach between pins 3 and 5.
7. **C0G's bias, temperature and ageing figures** are the EIA class's, not read from the Samsung datasheet *(check: not in the repository)*.
8. **CMRR at 3.3 V supply** (65 dB is specified at VS 5.4 V) and VOS across the input range (specified at one VCM): the crossover of the rail-to-rail input could add a step the linear calibration does not remove.
9. **Output swing near the rails at 3.3 V**: the datasheet gives 2/30 mV at 100 kOhm; the pin range needs 0.17 to 2.98 V, so it is not close, but it is not read at 3.3 V.
10. **Supply current**: 2 x 7.5 mA max at 3.3 V for the scope dual *(derived from IQ max, datasheet page 4)*.

**Spec questions still open (for the orchestrator)**

1. **Where SM-3's 8 MHz is read.** At the pin the stage is 40 MHz or better; at the Due's sampling capacitor the Due's own switch pole is 7.0 MHz at the derived 3.8 kohm / 6 pF. Confirm SM-3 is a pin figure (VT-16 measures it there).
2. **The pad budget.** The layout must hold 20 fF between pins 3 and 5 and 20 fF between the N nodes. If that is not credible, the circuit levers above (a lower Rnp, a larger Cpx) or one dual per channel (D16's fallback) are the choices.
3. **Rnp 470 ohm and the 10 mA note.** The margin is 0.9 mA at -12 V. A larger Rnp (560 ohm: 7.9 mA) needs its bandwidth, noise and phase margin re-run.
4. **Cn 12 pF** is a second C0G value (C38523) beside C1's 22 pF and Cpx's 10 pF; C1 20 pF with Cn 10 pF (the same part as Cpx) would drop one value at a 95th percentile of 6.5 % in place of 5.8 %. Confirm 12 pF.
